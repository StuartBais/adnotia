Adnotia · a direction for the phone app

Quarters

The day is four bold bands of colour — Morning, Midday, Afternoon and Evening — and the app opens in the band you are in, so the screen’s colour says where you are in the day.

  • Morninguntil 12:00Mist, ink 12.2:1
  • Midday12:00 to 14:00Mint, ink 11.2:1
  • Afternoon14:00 to 18:00Apricot, ink 11.6:1
  • Eveningfrom 18:00Dusk, ink 9.8:1

Colour is contentThe band is the ground; controls stay ink on every band, and white belongs to the one thing running and to capture. Each band is named in words and carries a sun-position glyph, so its colour is never the only cue.

Two scales of timeThe band says roughly when. Inside it, blocks of five minutes show what is left, ink for time still ahead, beside the number and the clock time it ends.

TypeOnest, variable 100–900, bundled. Bands 34/38 at 800, the focus step 34/40 at 800, body 17/24 at 450, meta 14/18 at 500. Proportional figures; row times sit in a fixed column.
