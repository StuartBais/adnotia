Adnotia · phone app · design direction

Lantern

A dark charcoal canvas lit by one sentence. The app opens on the current step alone, in very large white type, with a single terracotta bar burning down beneath it. The rest of the day waits folded in a light drawer at the bottom edge.

  • Charcoal #222624 canvas
  • White #FFFFFF
  • Ash #A9AFAB, added
  • Terracotta #F28C5E time
  • Stone #EEF0EC drawer
  • Sage #DFE9E0
  • Muted #595C5B, stone only

Instrument Sans, SIL Open Font License, variable weight 400–700, bundled in the app (loaded from Google Fonts for this mock-up). Step 40/46 and 44/50 at 700; nothing below 12 pt, no weight under 400. Text in the app measures 5.9:1 to 15.3:1; terracotta against its track 3.4:1.
