Roundels

Every task is a roundel: a white disc holding a drawn line object, inside a ring that is its duration. The running roundel sits large at the top of Today, and its ring empties as you work.

3FocusThe step is the largest thing on the screen. The clock is present, small, and beside its words.

One picture per task, picked from its words

About 60 on-device pictograms in one stroke. The person can change any of them, and no match gets a plain dot.

  • call
  • walk, park
  • permit
  • ink
  • note
  • laundry
  • no match

State is drawn as shape, never as colour alone

Each state also carries its words: “10 min”, “18 min left”, “Paused”, “Done”. Only a running roundel sits on a halo.
