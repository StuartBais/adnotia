Deck

The day is a hand of cards. The running task is the top card, the next ones fan out beneath it in the order of the day, and “Not now” slides a card to the back of the deck.

Type
Funnel Display for titles and the step, Funnel Sans for everything else. Both OFL, bundled.
Colour
Ground Cards, with ink #141518
A card’s colour only tells cards apart, like a folder’s. The glyph, title and time say what it is.
Motion
Cards travel on a spring, with a light haptic as one lifts and a firm one as it settles. Colour never springs. Reduce Motion cuts cards into place.
