# What belongs on the first screen?

**Reading date for every source below: 19 November 2026 (UTC).** This is a design study, not a ranking of treatments. I looked for the smallest unit that still makes an interface recognisable when you take its logo away. Then I looked for what happens when someone is interrupted, returns to a task, or gives the phone to someone else.

**Method.** I used web search for discovery, then opened source documents, published page text, maker documentation, an original screenshot and individual store reviews. Several publisher and storefront domains did not resolve from this environment. The links labelled **reading copy** are the actual texts I read, not links implying I visited the live sites. The store-award links identify the selections; a search excerpt alone does not substantiate any of the design lessons below. I didn't install or time these products. “Read” includes attributed criticism; “take” is my inference.

First, the product boundaries: [ADR-043](../../docs/decisions/ADR-043-a-phone-app-rebuilt-around-shared-domains.md), [ADR-044](../../docs/decisions/ADR-044-what-the-research-settled.md) and the [research report](../../reports/ADHD%20app%20design%20and%20tools.md) (summary, shared record, control/removal and drift sections). Fast capture and a non-accusing return have direct testimony behind them. One task record serving several views is a **design hypothesis**, not a user demand established by a trial. The trial evidence for a taught skills package does not transfer to a timer, a routine illustration or a phone theme.

## 1. Recent phone interfaces, outside as well as inside planning

The window here is November 2024–November 2026: dates of recognition or contemporary design study, not necessarily of first release. For the Design Awards I read the [2025–26 archive entries](https://github.com/M1zz/ADA/blob/main/docs/ada-archive.html) and the app maker's [field notes underlying those entries](https://github.com/M1zz/ADA/blob/main/docs/insights-data.js). These are a curator's observations, not Apple's research. The official catalogues are [2025](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025/) and [2026](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/).

| Interface and source | What I read | What I take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tide Guide**, 2026 ADA winner, archive above | The chart takes the screen, with observations aligned to the same time axis. Dragging along the curve reveals a value; the sky palette changes with the day. A widget can answer a smaller question without opening the whole chart. | The curve is not wallpaper. Even a small section of it tells you what this app does. Give remaining time a measurable shape **and** a literal value. Don't make people scrub for the answer. |
| **Mela**, 2025 interaction finalist, same archive | One recipe survives the change from reading to cooking. Cook Mode enlarges text and emphasises the current step rather than presenting the recipe website and its surroundings. | “Working on it” can be a different composition of the same words. Enlarge the active instruction; don't dim essential text below legibility. |
| **CapWords**, 2025 winner, same archive | The starting action is the camera. The photographed object becomes a cut-out labelled with a word; a short peel-like transition bridges recognition time. The saved thing looks like the input, not a new form. | Make the result of capture predictable. A visible task title can do the continuity work without waiting for an animation or a network. |
| **grug**, 2026 winner, [creators' documentation](https://github.com/ocho-git/HandDrawnFont/blob/main/README.md) and archive | The daily item is one piece of writing. Letters are made of real pen strokes, with multiple glyph variants, original stroke order, a static rendering and Reduce Motion support. Even ordinary lettering gives it an identity. | A distinctive typographic voice can do more than a page of decorations. Don't copy this hand or make anyone wait for letters to appear. |
| **Flighty**, studied by designers, [Spectr design study](https://github.com/Meliwat/awesome-ios-design-md/blob/main/design-md/travel/flighty/DESIGN.md) and maker's field notes above | The study singles out a route arc, paired airport identifiers, a large time, short labels and a compact gate/terminal group. On a smaller surface, which facts get the space changes with the phase of a flight. Its strong active accent works with direction and position. | A glanceable instrument is an editing decision, not a dark palette. Spend the largest numeral on the question a person is asking **now**. |
| **Tiimo**, 2025 iPhone App of the Year, [award report, full feed reading copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rlridenour/elfeed-db/HEAD/data/54/5417a17073b7a299a87841f78a17b2a9a888ea47) and archive | The award report names the pick. The design notes describe icon-labelled coloured blocks on a day line, a countdown and smaller steps within the current activity. What you can see first is when, not an inventory of tools. | Put the day before the tool catalogue; don't require a colour legend or an icon assignment to enter a task. |
| **Focus Friend**, [Google Play Best of 2025](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-apps-games-2025/), [Play review text, reading copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lalithsaicharan00/store-reviews/HEAD/Research/ADHD%20App%20Reviews/Play%20Store/59.%20Focus%20Friend%20by%20Hank%20Green/by_language/en.jsonl) | Google's listing surfaced the Best App pick. In the actual review text, one player describes leaving the running timer alone so a character can knit socks for room decorations. Another describes a session apparently forgotten when the phone locks. The work of the character makes the passage of time visible. | A timer can have a recognisable face without another obligation. Carry the time and task across interruptions; don't make stopping cost an earned object. |

| Reading copy | What people actually describe (benefit / friction) | Design consequence I infer |

| [Things 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lalithsaicharan00/store-reviews/HEAD/Research/ADHD%20App%20Reviews/App%20Store/53.%20Things%203%20-%20Organize%20your%20life/by_country/us.jsonl) | The inbox, widget/shortcut entry and focus on core functionality get praise. ## 3. Whose phone, whose view?

| Source opened | What I read or saw | What I would carry over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Joon, parents' own reviews](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lalithsaicharan00/store-reviews/HEAD/Research/ADHD%20App%20Reviews/App%20Store/10.%20Joon-%20Kids%20ADHD%20Chore%20Tracker%20-%20Family%20Chores%20Organizer%20Chart/by_country/us.jsonl) | A parent edits on a phone while their child checks off quests on an iPad. A parent who handed over their own phone found the game itself became the attraction. | Don't assume a second device, child login or connectivity. Hand over the activity, not the parent's planner or a game. |

| [STEPWISE, parent and child modes](https://github.com/MangYahel/STEPWISE/blob/HEAD/README.md); [Morning Routine, maker's guide](https://github.com/Racoon80/morning-routine/blob/HEAD/README.md) and [original active-step image](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Racoon80/morning-routine/HEAD/images/overlay-normal.png) | In one, the parent sends a saved sequence to a mode that presents one step at a time. ## 5. Which parts of today's look will wear out?

- **Glass.** Apple's [Materials text, reading copy](https://github.com/valentinllpz/apple-human-interface-guidelines/blob/main/references/materials.md) explicitly distinguishes a navigation layer from content and advises against glass in the content layer. A [Six Colors column, full feed reading copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rlridenour/elfeed-db/HEAD/data/d9/d99d12008e77b3853accd6b03078a4bd27a18df4) notices icon refraction and edge sharpness changing again in the next beta. **Judgement:** separating a control from what it controls will last; a hand-built imitation of this year's optics will not. Text shouldn't depend on what happens to scroll underneath.
- **Expressive shapes and spring motion.** Google's [icon-button guidance](https://github.com/material-components/material-components-android/blob/master/docs/components/IconButton.md) shows variations in size, shape and width; its motion page distinguishes spring speed by distance and component. **Judgement:** changing silhouette to mark purpose is useful. A different wobble for every tap, or a catalogue of novelty containers, makes all the controls compete. Keep the distinction at rest.
- **Animated personalities.** The grug maker documentation and Focus Friend reviews above give two very different examples: stroke rendering can express authorship; a companion can give someone a reason to put the phone down. **Judgement:** an identity on the thing a person came to do ages better than a dependent character with a second economy. A break must not cost anything.

## Ten principles to take into drawing

1. **Start in the middle of the day.** Lead with what is running. Put clock times beside the appointments and leave an explicitly untimed place for everything else. (Tiimo, Structured, Llama Life.)
2. **Give time two readings.** One shape for a glance; a number, unit and total for certainty. A marker must still make sense in greyscale. (Tide Guide, Flighty, HIG Color.)
3. **Carry the title, not a copy.** Let one item move from captured text to steps to timed work without another name or date. Test this hypothesis rather than calling it established. (CapWords, Mela, Marvin, ADR-044.)
4. **Capture needs one field and an exit.** No required date, tag, duration, account or setup. Say where it will go. (Things, W3C short paths.)
5. **Leave a place to resume.** Show the current step, not a history of unattended days. No automatic prompts or rewards. (Report on drift, Tiimo reviews, W3C reminders.)
6. **Change the scale when the job changes.** In Focus, remove other tasks rather than blurring them; make Pause easier to find than another tool. (Mela, Flighty.)
7. **Make the frame predictable.** Four named destinations, a separate Me entry, and few contextual verbs. Keep a direct way back from a detail or focused mode. (HIG, M3, Marvin reviews.)
8. **Separate the two audiences.** The parent gets the whole order and a named handoff; the child gets only the current instruction and what follows. No adult controls, child account or device-lock promise. (Joon, FocusKid, STEPWISE.)
9. **Express identity through structure.** Choose one repeatable relationship between a task's title, time and shape. Take neither another maker's objects nor their drawings. (grug, CapWords, Tide Guide.)
10. **Make the still frame sufficient.** Legible text, generous targets, visible focus, no colour-only state or motion-dependent feedback. Colour, type and light/dark treatments are identity choices, not ADHD interventions. (HIG, M3, W3C, report.)

**Ruled out:** a launch dashboard of equally weighted tools (delays returning); required scheduling (turns capture into planning); backlog badges, loss, resets and guilt (make returning expensive); hidden swipe-only actions (hard to discover); cloud suggestions and unsolicited nudges (take control and data away); glass-on-text, candy chips and infinite cards (decoration becomes the hierarchy). No screen, illustration or font from a studied app is reproduced.

## Second pass: what happens during an interruption?

**Additional reading, 19 November 2026.** The first plates left two questions unanswered: where does Save take me, and can I reach it while typing?

- **Read:** W3C's [Let Users Go Back](https://github.com/w3c/coga/blob/HEAD/design-guide/o4p02-back-undo.html) distinguishes returning to the same point from merely going home. Its example is returning to the same video after opening another view by mistake. **Inference:** the place to keep in Adnotia is a particular instruction in a particular session. Opening a running task must not be another Start button.
- **Read:** Apple's [Virtual keyboards](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/virtual-keyboards) ([page text read](https://github.com/valentinllpz/apple-human-interface-guidelines/blob/main/references/virtual-keyboards.md)) distinguishes keyboards by the content being entered and describes the Return key as part of the task. **Inference:** judge capture with the alphabetic keyboard taking up real space. Put the destination and Save beside the field, not below the keyboard, and give the keyboard's Done the same result as Save.
