1 and 4

Today and Focus, told apart

A risk with Lantern was that these two look alike. Each has what the other lacks. Today: the date, the stone drawer and the tabs. Focus: its name at the head of the context line, a bar run edge to edge like a horizon, and +5 min beside the minutes. On both, the step is the one white heading.

The row under the thumb is the same on both. While the capsule is opening, and for a second after either screen has grown out of it, Step done and Pause ignore taps, so a tap meant for the capsule cannot land on them.

3a and 3b, 6a and 6b

A choice for the owner: the other places in charcoal or in white

Charcoal, Lantern's default. One dark app, as Lantern drew it, and calmer at night. The capsule is not the one dark thing here, so it is lifted: a lighter fill and a full ash edge, with its Now and its burning bar. A task that is not running is charcoal too, close to the running task's own page. Long lists in white on charcoal may be harder to read in daylight.

White, Glasshouse's look. Charcoal keeps one meaning, now: the capsule is the one dark thing on a place, found at a glance, and the change of light says where you are. Moving between Today and a place is a big change of light, and it is bright at night.

Nothing else on the board depends on this choice. Stone stays the rest of today either way, and capture and the capsule do not move.

7, 7b and 7c · Weighed, not overlooked

While something runs, the rest of today stays folded

Whether that is enough is the first thing to test with people.

For this audience, a task out of sight is easy to lose track of. Lantern's fold is kept on purpose, so the step stays the one lit thing while it burns. When nothing runs, the day rests half open, as on 7.

What answers the risk: the Next line names what comes next and how soon on every tabbed screen, says Rest of today in words, and opens in one tap without putting the step out.

A timed item whose slot passes unstarted goes to the top of Anytime today, unmarked. The idle lantern lights it next. While a task runs or is paused, the Next line and Focus's foot name it, as on 7c, unless a timed item starts within 15 minutes or is under way.

After a tap

Every quick tap can be taken back

Lock Screen

The Lock Screen: the same object, at actual size

The step, the bar and the minutes, in the same order and colours as the capsule, and when the next booked item is. Step done works without unlocking, and a tap anywhere else opens Focus. There is no Pause here: pausing ends the card. Capture is the right-hand control, under the thumb as in the app; iOS lets the person change it.

System text (the status bar, the keyboard and this clock) is set in Inter, standing in for SF Pro. The wallpaper stands in for the person's own. The card's mark is the app's own, from assets/logo.svg.

Outside the app

Live Activity, Dynamic Island and Android

Drawn at actual size. The card starts on Start or Resume, and ends on Pause, on done, when another task starts, and with the logging day. The bar and the minutes are the system's own timers, so nothing has to be sent from a server. The Island's three states are in the header.
